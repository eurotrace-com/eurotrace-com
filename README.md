## About EuroTrace

**EuroTrace** provides cryptocurrency investigation, blockchain tracing, and recovery consulting services designed to help individuals understand and respond to cryptocurrency fraud and online financial scams.

Our approach focuses on analyzing available evidence, tracing digital asset movements, identifying potentially relevant transaction paths, and preparing structured investigation reports with practical action plans.

We help clients understand their cases and make informed decisions about potential next steps.

### What We Do

- **Cryptocurrency Fraud Investigation:** Examine available evidence relating to suspected cryptocurrency scams and fraudulent transactions.
- **Blockchain Forensics:** Analyze on-chain transaction activity and trace digital asset movements across wallets and relevant blockchain services.
- **Investigation Reports:** Organize findings into structured reports that may support discussions with exchanges, banks, legal professionals, or law enforcement.
- **Recovery Action Plans:** Provide step-by-step guidance on possible actions clients can take based on the investigation findings.
- **Expert Assistance:** Offer guidance to clients as they work through recommended next steps.
- **Confidential Case Handling:** Emphasize responsible document handling, confidentiality, and data protection.

Our objective is to make complex cryptocurrency investigations more understandable and help clients explore realistic options for pursuing their losses.

## How Our Investigation Process Works

Our process is designed to help clients move from an initial understanding of a suspected scam to a documented investigation and practical action plan.

1. **Case Review** — Conduct preliminary checks to assess the available information and potential investigation opportunities.
2. **Evidence Collection** — Gather relevant communications, transaction details, and other supporting documentation supplied by the client.
3. **Investigation** — Examine the available evidence and investigate relevant transaction activity.
4. **Investigation Report** — Present findings in a structured report.
5. **Action Plan** — Outline potential next steps based on the investigation.
6. **Expert Assistance** — Provide guidance as the client considers and carries out appropriate actions.

The outcome depends on the circumstances of each case. Cryptocurrency recovery is not guaranteed.
